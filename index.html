Educational Safety & Support

About "Substance X"

Substance X is a strictly fictional representation used to illustrate the physiological principles of cognitive impairment, reaction latency, and decision degradation without glorifying or detailing real-world drugs.

Refusal Strategies: The CLEAR Model

  • C - Calm: Keep a steady, confident posture and voice.
  • L - Look: Make direct eye contact without aggression.
  • E - Explain: Give a clear, straightforward "No, I'm good."
  • A - Alternative: Offer an alternate activity ("Let's grab a drink of water or head outside").
  • R - Remove: Politely step away from the pressurized setting.

Confidential Support Resources

If you or someone you know is facing substance pressure or challenges, confidential help is available 24/7:

  • SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357 (Free, confidential 24/7)
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
